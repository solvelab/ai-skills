# Fair-weather cumulus

regimes: `advected-field`
view: from below and to the side, horizon in frame — the flat base is the recognisable feature and
it only reads with the horizon present

| fact | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| base height | 125 m per °C of dewpoint spread | Espy's equation |
| bases across a field | ALL at the same level — they breathe the same air | consequence of the above, and the most recognisable fact about a cumulus field |
| perimeter fractal dimension | ≈ 1.35 | Lovejoy 1982 |
| cell lifetime | 10-20 min for humilis; grows fast, decays slow | cloud physics |
| wind at 1.5 km | ~12 m/s typical | meteorology |
| shading | white top, grey base — light is scattered out on the way down through the cloud's depth | radiative |

The base is a CUT through the bubbles at the condensation level, not a slab drawn beneath them.

# One word per line, lowercase. Every entry must be a word that is English and NOT Portuguese: the
# detector asserts, at load and in --selftest, that this file and prose-words-pt.txt share no word.
# Words both languages carry (`a`, `as`, `no`, `do`, `me`, `sem`, `via`, `so`, `for`, `note`,
# `data`, `total`, `final`, `real`, `local`, `etc`, `ok`, `set`, `value`, `use`, `come`) are
# deliberately absent, and so are the words a loop or a variable is named with (`i`, `x`, `num`,
# `val`) — see prose-words.SOURCE.md.

# Rain

regimes: `ballistic-ensemble`

| fact | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| terminal velocity | `v = 9.65 − 10.3·exp(−0.6·d)` m/s, d in mm | Gunn & Kinzer |
| checks | 0.5 mm → 2.02, 1 mm → 4.00, 2 mm → 6.55, 4 mm → 8.72, 6 mm → 9.37 m/s | against published 2-9 m/s for 0.6-4 mm |
| size distribution | exponential; small drops vastly outnumber large | Marshall-Palmer |
| streak length | velocity × exposure — motion blur, not a style parameter | photographic |

One root quantity: the diameter sets size, speed, streak and opacity together, and they cannot
contradict each other.
